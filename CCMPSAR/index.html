Our commitment

We understand the unimaginable pain of waiting. Our volunteers search land, river and sea to give families answers, closure and support.

Set up by members of the Cork community, the team is available 365 days a year. We work closely with the Gardaí, the Coast Guard and local communities. Our service is free of charge, because no family should have to search alone.

First steps

If someone you love is missing

Act quickly. These steps help the Gardaí and the search teams from the very start.

  1. Ring the Gardaí

    999 or 112
    Report it straight away. There is no 24-hour wait.

  2. Have a recent photo ready

    Plus their height, build, hair and what they were last wearing.

  3. Note where and when

    Where they were last seen, who they were with, and places they like to go.

  4. You are not alone

    Once a search is under way, our family liaison team can be your point of contact.

What we do

Our operations

Specialist training and deep local knowledge let our units work across challenging ground and water.

Land searches

Organised urban and rural searches, coordinating large groups of volunteers with specialised mapping equipment.

River and coastal

Operations along the River Lee and the Cork coastline, using our rigid inflatable boats (RIBs) and side-scan sonar.

Family liaison

A dedicated, compassionate point of contact, so families are kept informed, supported and respected throughout.
