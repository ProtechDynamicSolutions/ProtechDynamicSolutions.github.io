How we work

How a switchgear job runs

The same order on every job, so you know what happens next and nothing is energised before it has been checked.

  1. Survey

    We visit site, review drawings and the existing installation, and agree the scope.

  2. Specify and supply

    Equipment is specified to the design and procured to suit the programme.

  3. Install

    Switchgear is positioned, terminated and wired with the site kept safe and tidy.

  4. Test and commission

    Everything is tested and set up before it goes live, with results recorded.

  5. Maintain

    Planned maintenance and fast fault finding for the life of the equipment.
