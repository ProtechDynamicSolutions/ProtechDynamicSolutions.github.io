Sample Coach Tour Itinerary | 4 Days Galway & Connemara

Tour Route & Highlights

 Travel the Island of Ireland
Luxury Coach Throughout
Full Irish Breakfasts x3 Included

Explore Galway & Connemara | 4 Day Itinerary

Day 1 | Depart for Galway Collection from your specified collection point.
Transfer to Galway for check-in to the 3-Star Salthill Hotel.
Dinner at the Hotel with live entertainment.
Day 2 | Exploring Connemara Full Irish Breakfast at your hotel.
Full day tour of beautiful Connemara.
Free time in Clifden for a stroll and lunch.
Visit to Kylemore Abbey and Garden.
Dinner and Overnight at your hotel.
Day 3 | The Burren & Cliffs of Moher Full Irish Breakfast at your hotel.
Full Tour of the Burren Region.
Lunch – enjoyed en route.
Visit the Cliffs of Moher & Visitor Centre.
Return to Hotel for dinner with live entertainment.
Day 4 | Departure Full Irish Breakfast at your hotel.
Depart Galway for the journey home.
