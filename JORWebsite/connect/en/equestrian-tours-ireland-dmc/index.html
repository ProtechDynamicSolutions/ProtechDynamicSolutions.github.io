Sample Itinerary | Equestrian Trip in Ireland

Tour Route & Highlights

 4x Full Days of Hunting
Stay in an Iconic Country Manor
Authentic Irish Evening Entertainment

Horse & Hound Trip– 7 Day Itinerary

Day 1 | Arrival Dublin Arrive to Dublin Airport, meet with your experienced Tour Guide and escorted to your awaiting coach.
Surprise welcome for your group with a Bagpiper and a welcome whiskey.
Breakfast at your hotel.
Guided Tour of the seaside village of Howth.
Visit to Newgrange a cultural site from 3,200 BC.
Pre-Drinks in Clarke's Pub.
Dinner in Clarke's Pub.
Live show of traditional Irish music and dance.
Overnight at Hanover House, a unique period country house built in 1690.
Day 2 | Hunting Traditional Irish Breakfast at your hotel.
Depart accommodation for the Hunt with Fingal Harriers Hunting Club.
Lunch at a local Irish pub.
Begin the hunt - 3.5 Hrs approx.
Soup and sandwiches at a traditional Irish pub.
Enjoy dinner at The Snailbox, a traditional Irish restaurant.
Return to accommodation and enjoy an evening of traditional Irish music.
Overnight at Mount Hanover House.
Day 3 | Hunting Traditional Irish Breakfast at your hotel.
Depart accommodation for the Hunt with Tara Harriers Hunting Club.
Lunch at a local Irish pub.
Begin the hunt - 3.5 Hrs approx.
Soup and sandwiches at a traditional Irish pub.
Enjoy dinner at O'Brien's, a traditional Irish restaurant.
Return to accommodation.
Overnight at Mount Hanover House.
Day 4 | Exploring Dublin Traditional Irish Breakfast at your hotel.
Guided Visit of the Guinness Storehouse.
Lunch at the Guinness Storehouse.
Visit to the Irish Emigration Museum.
Visit to Trinity College & The Book of Kells.
A Whiskey Blending Experience at the Irish Whiskey Museum.
A Night of Dog Racing and Dinner in Shelbourne Park.
After Dinner Drinks in Café en Seine.
Return to accommodation.
Overnight at Mount Hanover House.
Day 5 | Hunting Traditional Irish Breakfast at your hotel.
Depart accommodation for the Hunt with Fingal Harriers Hunting Club.
Lunch at a local Irish pub.
Begin the hunt - 3.5 Hrs approx.
Soup and sandwiches at a traditional Irish pub.
Enjoy dinner in Man O’War, with an Irish Coffee Demonstration.
Return to accommodation.
Overnight at Mount Hanover House.
Day 6 | Hunting Traditional Irish Breakfast at your hotel.
Depart accommodation for the Hunt with Oriel Farney Harriers.
Lunch at a local Irish pub.
Begin the hunt - 3.5 Hrs approx.
Soup and sandwiches at a traditional Irish pub.
Pre-Dinner Whiskey Tasting in Slane Distillery.
Enjoy dinner in Browne’s Bar in Slane Castle.
Return to accommodation for an evening of live traditional Irish music.
Overnight at Mount Hanover House.
Day 7 | Dublin & Departure Traditional Irish Breakfast at your hotel.
Check Out & Coach Transfer to Dublin Airport for departure.
