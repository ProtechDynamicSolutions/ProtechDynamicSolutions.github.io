Sample Luxury FIT Itinerary

Tour Route & Highlights

 Explore the Wild Atlantic Way
Experience the Best of Ireland
5-Star Hotels, Castles & Manors

8 Days | The Luxury Tour of Ireland & The Wild Atlantic Way

Day 1 | Dublin & Welcome Arrival Dublin Airport to the care of your experienced Chauffeur–Guide, and enjoy a welcome shot of Irish Whiskey.
Visit to Trinity College, known for its treasures, in particular the famous Book of Kells, an illuminated manuscript of the 9th century.
Lunch in a traditional Dublin pub.
Visit to Guinness Storehouse, the home of Guinness Stout, the famous 'black stuff'.
Dinner at Dublin's famous Ivy Restaurant.
Overnight at the 5-Star Westin Hotel in Dublin City Centre.
Day 2 | Whiskey Secrets & Galway Full Irish Breakfast at your hotel.
Your Self-Drive Car is delivered to your hotel.
Depart for County Galway, traversing the central plains of Ireland, East to West.
Connoisseur Experience at Kilbeggan Distillery, and discover how Irish whiskey is made since 1757 in a unique and exclusive ‘All Access’ tour with a whiskey expert.
Lunch – enjoyed en route.
Continue on to the famous village of Cong, County Mayo.
Dinner in Collins' Cottage at Ashford Castle.
Overnight at the 5-Star Ashford Castle Hotel, Cong.
Day 3 | The Magic of Connemara Full Irish Breakfast at the castle.
Full-Day Excursion of Connemara for its wild and rugged landscape of lakes and bogs, mountains and valleys, cliffs and beaches.
Visit to Kylemore Abbey & Gardens, the world-famous Bendictine Monastery.
Cruise on Killary Fjord, with lunch on board.
Visit to Connemara Oyster Farm with tasting.
Continue on to the famous village of Cong, County Mayo.
Dinner at Pat Cohan's Bar in Cong Village.
Overnight at the 5-Star Ashford Castle.
Day 4 | Ashford Castle Demesne Full Irish Breakfast at the castle.
Partake in Country Pursuits on Ashford Castle Estate – I.E falconry, fishing, horseriding, golf, and more.
Afternoon treatments at hotel spa.
Dinner in the George V Dining Room at Ashford Castle.
Overnight at the 5-Star Ashford Castle Hotel, Cong.
Day 5 | Burren & Cliffs of Moher Full Irish Breakfast at the castle.
Full Day Exploration to The Burren and Cliffs of Moher.
Cruise - enjoy views of the incredible Cliffs of Moher from the Atlantic.
Lunch – enjoyed en route.
Visit to Bunratty Castle & Folk Park with tea / coffee and scones.
Dinner at The Oak Room Restaurant at the Manor.
Overnight at the 5-Star Adare Manor.
Day 6 | Dingle Peninsula Full Irish Breakfast at the manor.
Full Day Exploration of the Dingle Peninsula, a region renowned for its scenic coastal road along the Atlantic Ocean.
Lunch – enjoyed en route.
Visit to the Gallarus Oratory.
Irish Coffee Demonstration at a traditional Irish Pub.
Dinner at the The Carriage House in Adare Manor.
Overnight at the 5-Star Adare Manor.
Day 7 | Country Pursuits – Cashel Full Irish Breakfast at the manor.
Depart for Cashel.
Full Day to explore the activities, both indoor and outdoor, at Adare Manor. These include horse-drawn carriage ride, cycling, horse-riding, golf, and fishing.
Lunch at the clubhouse.
Visit to the Rock of Cashel.
Dinner at The Bishop's Buttery within Cashel Palace Manor.
Overnight at the brand new 5-Star Cashel Palace Manor.
Day 8 | Adare – Dublin – Depart Full Irish Breakfast at the manor.
Depart Adare towards Dublin Airport for departure flight.
