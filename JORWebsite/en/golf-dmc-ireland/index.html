Why Take Your Golf Trip to Ireland?

Only in Ireland can your crew sink a putt overlooked by a Viking tower, break for oysters, and spend the night in an 8th-Century castle.

Sample Itinerary: 6-Day Ultimate Dublin-Based Golf Trip

Tour Route & Highlights

 Stay in the 5-Star Shelbourne Hotel
 Play 5 Rounds of Golf
Evening of Greyhound Racing in Dublin
Welcome Whiskey on Arrival

Ultimate Irish Golf Trip – 6 Day Itinerary

Day 1 | The Island Golf Links, Dublin Arrive to Dublin Airport, meet with your experienced Tour Guide and escorted to your awaiting coach.
Surprise welcome for your group with a Bagpiper and a welcome whiskey.
Early lunch at the Island Golf Club Restaurant.
Round of Golf at the Island Golf Links Course.
Panoramic Tour of Dublin City.
Welcome cocktail on check-in.
Private Pub Crawl across 3 of Dublin’s finest traditional pubs, finishing with dinner, music, and dance at the famous Oliver St John Gogarty’s Pub in Temple Bar.
Overnight with Full Irish Breakfast at the 5-Star City Centre Shelbourne Hotel.
Day 2 | Baltray Golf Links (County Louth) Round of Golf at Baltray Golf Links.
Late lunch at the Clubhouse Restaurant in Baltray, winner of the Best Golf Club restaurant in 2017.
Transfer back to Dublin City Centre.
Private Whiskey Workshop at the Old Jameson Distillery, and learn how to blend your very own Whiskey.
Evening of Dinner and Greyhound Racing at Shelbourne Park Greyhound Stadium, situated in the city centre, including a sponsored race and some betting tickets.
Overnight with Full Irish Breakfast at the 5-Star Shelbourne Hotel.
Day 3 | Palmer South Ryder Cup Course Round of Golf at the Palmer South Ryder Cup “Parkland Course” at the K Club Golf Resort.
Late lunch at Cliff at Lyons Orangery, close to the K Club, followed by an Irish Coffee making demonstration.
Transfer back into Dublin City Centre.
Guided Tour of the iconic Guinness Storehouse with a private connoisseur experience, and print your own photo souvenir on your pint of Guinness.
Dinner at Sophie's Restaurant, Dublin’s unique rooftop restaurant with a traditional cocktail served on arrival.
Overnight with Full Irish Breakfast at the 5-Star Shelbourne Hotel.
Day 4 | Druids Glen Golf Course Round of Golf at Druids Glen Golf “Parkland” Course.
Late lunch at the Delgany Pigeon House Restaurant.
Return to Dublin with a surprise “Musical Hitchhiker” en route, and a shot of whiskey!
Tour and recording of a piece of music accompanied by a musician in the private studios at Windmill Lane, famous thanks to the group U2.
Dine in Cleaver East, one of the best steakhouses in Dublin which is located in the U2-owned hotel in the heart of Temple Bar.
Overnight with Full Irish Breakfast at the 5* Shelbourne Hotel.
Day 5 | Old Portmarnock Links Course Round of Golf at the Old Portmarnock Links Course.
Late lunch in the little fishing village of Howth, at the Panoramic Seafood Restaurant Aqua, situated on the water. Oyster tasting with a Guinness on arrival.
Pub Games in a traditional Irish Pub.
Pre-dinner cocktail at the Horse Shoe Bar at hotel.
Farewell dinner in the private and iconic Constitution Room at the Shelbourne Hotel where the Constitution of Ireland was signed.
Afterparty at some of Dublin's top pubs beside the hotel.
Overnight with Full Irish Breakfast at the 5* Shelbourne Hotel.
Day 6 | Departure Free time in the city centre before transferring to Dublin Airport for departure.
