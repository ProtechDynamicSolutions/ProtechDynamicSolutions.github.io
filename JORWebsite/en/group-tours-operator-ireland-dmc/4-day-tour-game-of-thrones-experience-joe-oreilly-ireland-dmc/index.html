Game of Thrones Experience – 4 Day Itinerary

Day 1 | Arrival – Dublin – Belfast Arrive to Dublin Airport, meet with your experienced Tour Guide and escorted to your awaiting coach.
Welcome shot of Irish whiskey.
Depart for Belfast, travelling via the Boyne Valley.
Visit Tollymore Forest, film location for Game of Thrones.
Lunch in an Irish pub.
Visit the ruins of Inch Abbey.
Continue to Belfast City Capital of Northern Ireland.
Dinner and Overnight at your hotel in the Belfast region.
Day 2 | Co. Antrim & Giant’s Causeway Full Day Tour of the North Antrim Coast.
Stop at Carnlough Harbour - film location for Game of Thrones.
Lunch in an Irish pub.
Visit Cushendun Caves - film location for Game of Thrones.
Visit to the Giant’s Causeway.
Stop at the Dark Hedges.
Dinner and Overnight at your hotel in the Belfast region.
Day 3 | Belfast – Co. Down – Dublin Panoramic Tour of Belfast City and environs.
Lunch in an Irish Pub.
Visit to Winterfell Castle – film location for Game of Thrones.
Archery.
Depart for Dublin.
Dinner and Overnight at your hotel in the Dublin region.
Day 4 | Dublin & Departure Panoramic Tour of Dublin via luxury coach.
Afternoon free for lunch and sightseeing or shopping.
Transfer to Dublin Airport for departure.
