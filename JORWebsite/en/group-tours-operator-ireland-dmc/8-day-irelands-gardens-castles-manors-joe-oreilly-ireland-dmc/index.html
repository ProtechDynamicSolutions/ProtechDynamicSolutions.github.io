Explore Ireland’s Gardens, Castles & Manors – 8 Day Itinerary

Day 1 | Arrive to Dublin Arrive to Dublin Airport, meet with your experienced Tour Guide and escorted to your awaiting coach.
Welcome shot of Irish whiskey.
Lunch – enjoyed en route.
Panoramic Tour of Dublin City.
Visit to the Botanic Gardens in Glasnevin.
Visit to the Phoenix Park.
Dinner and Overnight at your hotel in Dublin.
Day 2 | Wicklow – The Garden of Ireland Travel to County Wicklow, known as the Garden of Ireland.
Explore the gardens of Powerscourt House.
Lunch – enjoyed en route.
Visit to Mount Usher Gardens.
Dinner and Overnight at your hotel in Kilkenny.
Day 3 | From Kilkenny to Cork Visit to Kilkenny Castle.
Lunch – enjoyed en route.
Visit to the Rock of Cashel.
Visit to Cahir Castle.
Dinner and Overnight at your hotel in Cork.
Day 4 | Gardens of Cork & Kerry Explore Blarney Castle & Guided Visit of the Gardens.
Panoramic Tour of Cork City.
Travel to County Kerry.
Lunch – enjoyed en route.
Visit to Muckross Gardens in the Killarney National Park.
Visit to Muckross House.
Dinner and Overnight at your hotel in Kerry.
Day 5 | Gardens of Clare Travel to County Clare.
Explore Bunratty Castle & Folk Park.
Lunch – enjoyed en route.
Explore the Cliffs of Moher.
Dinner and Overnight at your hotel in Clare.
Day 6 | Stunning Connemara Full Day Exploration of stunning Connemara.
Explore St Brigit’s Gardens.
Lunch – enjoyed en route.
Visit to Kylemore Abbey & its Victorian Gardens.
Experience Killary Fjord.
Dinner and Overnight at your hotel in Clare.
Day 7 | Castle Gardens Travel to County Offaly.
Visit the Gardens of Birr Castle, home of the 7th Earl of Rosse.
Lunch – enjoyed en route.
Visit to the Gardens of Belvedere House.
Dinner and Show with Traditional Irish Music & Dance.
Overnight at your hotel in Dublin.
Day 8 | Shopping & Departure Free time for shopping in Dublin.
Transfer to Dublin Airport for departure.
