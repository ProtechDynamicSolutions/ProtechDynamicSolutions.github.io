Best of the Wild Atlantic Way – 8 Day Itinerary

Whole tour
Day1

Day 1 | Arrive to Shannon

Arrive to Shannon Airport, meet with your experienced Tour Guide and escorted to your awaiting coach.
Welcome shot of Irish whiskey.
Lunch – enjoyed en route.
Travel to County Clare.
Explore Bunratty Castle and Folk Park.
Dinner and Overnight at your hotel in Clare.
Day2

Day 2 | Explore the Aran Islands

Sail to Inis Mór – the largest of the Aran Islands.
Tour of the Island by mini bus on arrival.
Explore the ancient Dun Aonghasa Fort.
Lunch – enjoyed en route.
Return to Doolin in the late afternoon.
Dinner and Overnight at your hotel in the Clare region.
Day3

Day 3 | Exploring Connemara

Full Day Exploration of the incredible landscape of Connemara.
Lunch – enjoyed en route.
Photo Stop at Kylemore Abbey.
Visit to the Leenane Sheep & Woollen Mill in Leenane.
Walking Tour of Galway City, Ireland’s cultural capital.
Dinner and Overnight at your hotel in Clare.
Day4

Day 4 | The Burren & Cliffs of Moher

Full Day Exploration of the Burren Region.
Visit to the Poulnabrone Dolmen.
Explore the Cliffs of Moher.
Cruise around the Cliffs of Moher.
Lunch – enjoyed en route.
Sail across the Shannon Estuary from Killimer to Tarbert.
Dinner and Overnight at your hotel in Kerry.
Day5

Day 5 | Dingle Peninsula

Full Day Tour of the Dingle Peninsula.
Lunch – enjoyed en route.
Visit to the Ruins of Kilmalkedar Church.
Visit to the Gallarus Oratory.
Irish Coffee in an Irish Pub
Dinner and Overnight at your hotel in Kerry.
Day6

Day 6 | The Ring of Kerry

Full Day Exploration of the Ring of Kerry.
Sheepdog Demonstration with authentic Gaelic-speaking shepard.
Lunch – enjoyed en route.
Visit to Molls Gap and Ladies’ View.
Visit Muckross Gardens in the Killarney National Park.
Jaunting Car Trip in the Killarney National Park.
Travel to Cork City.
Dinner and Overnight at your hotel in Cork.
Day7

Day 7 | Cork – Limerick

Panoramic Tour of Cork City.
Visit to Blarney Castle & Gardens.
Lunch – enjoyed en route.
Visit to the Jameson Distillery.
Travel to Limerick.
Dinner and Overnight at your hotel in Limerick.
Day8

Day 8 | Shopping & Departure

Free time for shopping in Limerick.
Transfer to Shannon Airport for departure.
