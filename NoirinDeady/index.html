Questions people ask

Anything else, just ask. There’s no such thing as a silly question.

Is coaching the same as counselling?

No. Coaching mostly looks forward, at where you are now and what you want to change. If something comes up that needs a counsellor or therapist, I’ll tell you straight and help you find the right person.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on you. Some people come for a few sessions to work through one thing, others stay longer. We’ll talk about it at the start and you’re never locked in.

Is it confidential?

Yes. What you say stays between us. The only exception is the rare case where someone’s safety is at risk, and I’d always talk to you first.

Does online work as well as in person?

For most people, yes. You’re in your own space, there’s no travel, and it’s easier to fit around work and family. All you need is a quiet room and a phone or laptop.

What happens on the free chat?

We talk for about twenty minutes. You tell me a bit about what’s going on and I explain how I work. If it feels right, we book a first session. If not, no hard feelings.

Let’s have a chat.

The first chat is free and there’s no pressure. Tell me a little about what’s going on and I’ll get back to you within a day or two.
