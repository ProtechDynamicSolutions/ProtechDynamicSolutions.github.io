Who we are

Based in Cork, Ireland, Oakwood Construction Consultants Ltd. provides expert quantity surveying and commercial management services. We bridge the gap between architectural vision and financial reality, ensuring that every Euro is accounted for and maximised.

How we do it

Construction projects are complex, but cost control shouldn't be. Through rigorous measurement, proactive procurement strategies, and robust contract administration, we mitigate financial risks. From bespoke residential builds to multi-million Euro commercial developments, we safeguard your budget from inception to the final account.

Our Expertise

Professional QS Services

Delivering cost certainty and value engineering at every stage of the construction lifecycle.

Cost Planning & Estimating

Accurate and realistic preliminary cost advice. We develop detailed cost plans to establish feasible budgets in Euros, giving you financial clarity before design works are fully committed.

Bills of Quantities

Preparation of precise and comprehensive Bills of Quantities (BoQ) in accordance with ARM4, allowing for accurate contractor pricing and streamlined evaluation.

Value Engineering

Systematic reviews to improve value. We identify alternative methods and materials to reduce lifecycle costs without compromising the quality, functionality, or aesthetic of the build.

Tendering & Procurement

Managing the entire tender process. We draft tender documents, advise on the most suitable procurement routes, and provide detailed analytical reports on contractor submissions.

Contract Administration

Acting as the independent certifier. We prepare contract documents (RIAI, Public Works), value monthly progress payments, and rigorously assess any claims for variations or delays.

Final Account Settlement

Bringing projects to a successful financial close. We meticulously review all variations, provisional sums, and dayworks to negotiate and agree on a fair and conclusive final account.
