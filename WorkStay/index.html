FAQ

Questions we get asked.

Can't see your question? Call or email us and we'll answer it.

Can we book a whole building?

Yes. Newgrange Lodge sleeps up to 80 and An Grianán has 80 beds, so either one can take a full crew on its own.

What's the shortest stay you take?

We're set up for stays of a week or more, right up to a year. Ask us about shorter stays and we'll see what we can do.

Do you invoice the company?

Yes. Most bookings are made by employers, and we invoice your company weekly or monthly.

Is there parking for vans?

Newgrange Lodge and An Grianán both have large on-site car parks. Lismar House is in Dundalk town centre, so ask us about parking there.

Can people cook for themselves?

Yes. Newgrange Lodge has two fitted kitchens with dining rooms, and An Grianán has a full catering kitchen.

Are bills included?

Yes. Heat, electricity and Wi-Fi are included in the rate.

Where exactly are you?

Newgrange Lodge, Donore, Co. Meath, A92 PP89. An Grianán, Termonfeckin, Co. Louth, A92 F443. Lismar House, 8/9 Stapleton Place, Dundalk, A91 E223.
